Services

Solar-electric boats, with full support
Coron, Palawan & across the Philippines.


ESMI works directly with island-hopping and dive tour operators, resorts, LGUs and NGOs. From the first conversation to years on the water, we support you at every step: choosing the right boat, financing it, registering it, training your crew and keeping it running.

Our boats replace fuel with sunlight: near-zero fuel costs, quiet and low-vibration trips, and a cleaner image for your business, in a boat your captains and passengers quickly feel at home with.

We start in Coron, Palawan and are expanding to El Nido and other tourism hubs, so every operator we work with has local support close by.

Consultation
& configuration

We visit your dock, look at your routes, passenger numbers and charging options, and listen to how you run your trips. We then propose the right configuration: compact, standard or extended up to six seats, with the finish and colours of your choice.

Together we compare your current fuel spend with the cost of an ESMI boat, including financing, leasing or rent-to-own options.

Visualisation:
See your boat first

Before anything is built, we prepare 3D visuals of your configured boat so you can check every detail: seating, solar roof, helm console and colours.

For resorts and larger fleets we also produce 3D videos to share with your team, partners or local authorities.

Delivery, registration
& training

Your boat is delivered in modules and assembled on site by our technicians. We guide you through MARINA registration and the paperwork your boat needs to operate, with Coast Guard safety equipment built in.

At handover we train your captain and crew on driving, charging and daily checks, and our service plans cover battery health checks, system updates and battery replacement over the years.
