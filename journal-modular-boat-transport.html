How a modular boat travels

Many of the islands that need clean boats have no boatyard. That is why the ESMI boat is designed to come apart, travel light, and be put back together where it will work.

Built from
modules

The hulls, deck panels, roof posts, solar roof, seats and helm console are separate modules joined with bolted connections. Taken apart, they can be packed on a truck or in a shipping container, travel by road and ferry, and be delivered to the customer's door.

Assembled
on the shore

On arrival, a small ESMI team assembles the boat at the water's edge: hulls first, then the deck, posts and solar roof, and finally the seats and helm.

The owner's crew can watch and learn as the boat comes together, which makes everyday checks and later servicing much more familiar.

. ready for the ferry .

Easy to store,
easy to repair

The same design helps long after delivery. In typhoon season or the quiet months, the boat can be taken apart and stored indoors, away from storms and salt. And a damaged part can be replaced on its own, instead of repairing a whole hull.

. delivered to the door .
