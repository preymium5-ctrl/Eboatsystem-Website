About

ESMI builds modular, solar-powered electric boats for the fishers and coastal communities of the Philippines.


Founded in November 2025 by June Delgado, ESMI, Eboat System Merchandising Incorporated, was born from a simple conviction: the people who live from the sea deserve boats that are cleaner, quieter and cheaper to run.

Every ESMI boat is designed to be shipped in parts, delivered to the door and assembled on site. Its solar roof powers a quiet electric drive, so there is no fuel to buy and far less to maintain.

The deck is modular: it can be widened to carry up to six seats, fitted out for fishing, island transport or tourism, and finished in the colour of your choice.

It started with listening: field surveys in Coron, Palawan

Before drawing a single line, June and his team travelled across Coron and the island communities of Palawan to meet local fishers and boat operators. They listened to their daily realities: fuel costs eating into every catch, engines that are hard to repair far from a workshop, and fragile reefs affected by noise and fuel spills.

Back at the drawing table, those conversations became a clear plan: a boat that is simple to transport, easy to assemble and maintain, and powered entirely by the sun.

From plan to shoreline: putting the first boats in the water

Executing the plan means being there. The ESMI team delivers each boat in modules, assembles it on the beach together with the community, and hands it over with practical training on everyday use and maintenance.

We work hand in hand with local fishers, cooperatives and tourism operators, so that every boat helps lower daily costs, protect the waters families depend on, and build a more sustainable livelihood for the next generation.
