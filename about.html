About

ESMI develops modular solar-electric boats, solar-powered boat engines and plug-and-play propulsion systems for fishermen, boat owners, tourism operators and coastal communities across the Philippines.


Founded in October 2025 by June Delgado and Herbert Villadelrey and officially registered with the SEC on January 29, 2026, ESMI (Eboat System Merchandising Incorporated) is driven by a simple vision: cleaner, quieter, more economical and sustainable boating.

Every ESMI boat is designed to be delivered in modules and assembled on site. Powered by renewable solar energy, it eliminates diesel and petrol consumption during electric operation, reduces maintenance costs and minimises noise and water pollution.

Its innovative modular deck can be expanded in both length and width, accommodating more passengers depending on water conditions, intended application and safety requirements. Each boat can be customised for fishing, tourism, transportation or leisure.
