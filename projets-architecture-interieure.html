Projects

  • Silent & fuel-free Island-hopping tours - Coron, Palawan

    Fuel is one of the largest daily costs for tour operators. ESMI boats run on sunlight, cutting fuel spend to near zero while giving guests a quieter, fume-free trip between lagoons and reefs.

  • Quiet & reef-friendly Dive and snorkel boats - El Nido

    No exhaust and low vibration over the reef: a calmer experience for divers and a cleaner one for the coral that dive businesses depend on.

  • Premium & on-brand Guest shuttles for island resorts

    A visibly sustainable guest transfer that matches a resort's eco promise. One partner, several boats, with crew training and service included.

  • Essential & reliable Medical and goods transport for remote islands

    For local governments and NGOs: a commercially available boat for inter-island medical deliveries, goods transport and disaster response, ready to deploy across many communities, not just one.

  • Local & affordable Coastal communities and local fishers

    Lease and rent-to-own options priced against today's fuel spend, so more families can switch to clean, low-cost boats.

  • Tested & measured Pilot deployments with partner operators

    Two-to-four-week pilots with resorts and tour operators, tracking real range, charging, passenger loads and uptime before each wider rollout.

  • Accessible & flexible Financing, leasing and rent-to-own

    We work with rural banks and leasing partners so operators don't need the full price upfront, with monthly payments designed around the fuel they no longer buy.

  • Registered & compliant MARINA registration and safety support

    Guidance on vessel registration and the Certificate of Public Convenience, with Coast Guard safety built in: life-vest mounting, capacity plating and signage.

  • Maintained & supported Service, training and battery care

    Battery health checks, system updates and a battery replacement program, plus training for operators' own crews on everyday maintenance.

Projects
& roadmap

ESMI builds solar-electric boats for Philippine marine tourism and transport, designed for the operators who keep the islands moving: island-hopping and dive tour operators first, then resorts, local governments and community programs.

Why now. Fuel has become one of the largest daily costs for boat operators. In April 2026, a pumpboat operator in Cebu reported that fuel for a 10-litre fill had tripled, taking roughly 30% of a ₱5,000 trip's revenue (Rappler). At the same time, Palawan welcomed more than 2.1 million tourists in 2025 (Palawan Provincial Tourism Office), and destinations like El Nido and Coron face growing pressure to protect their waters.

Phase 1, Coron: pilot deployments and our first anchor customers, with financing and a local service model in place.
Phase 2, El Nido and new hubs: expansion across Palawan and the Visayas, smaller and more affordable models, and financing partnerships with banks and leasing companies.
Phase 3, communities and LGUs: inter-island medical and goods transport and disaster response.
Phase 4, Southeast Asia: island tourism markets across the region and the Pacific that use similar boats.

Every ESMI boat comes with MARINA registration guidance, crew training and battery care, so operators can make the switch with confidence.
