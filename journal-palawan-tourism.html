Palawan's tourism boom and the case for quieter boats

Palawan welcomed more than 2.1 million tourists in 2025, according to the Palawan Provincial Tourism Office: 821,504 arrivals in Puerto Princesa, 642,385 in El Nido and 336,304 in Coron.

A decade
of growth

Asian Development Bank data show arrivals in Coron and El Nido growing from 76,815 in 2008 to over 900,000 in 2019, roughly 25% a year, and growth has continued since the pandemic. In El Nido alone, around 600 tourist boats are registered (Philippine Information Agency, 2025).

Pressure
on the water

Overtourism and water quality in El Nido are now under scrutiny from government and industry.

Every extra trip by a fuel-powered bangka adds noise, fumes and the risk of spills in the same lagoons and reefs that draw visitors in the first place.

Growing without
the noise

Quiet, fuel-free boats let operators offer the premium, eco-conscious experience many travellers now look for, while protecting the places their business depends on. That is the opportunity ESMI is building for, starting in Coron.
