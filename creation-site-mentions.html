Personal data

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not collect personal data while you browse.
The 3D viewer on the Services page remembers the colours you choose for the boat in your own browser (local storage). This information stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be cleared from your browser settings at any time.
If you write to us by email, we use your message and contact details only to reply to you, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). To ask us to access, correct or delete this information, write to info@eboatsystem.com.

Boats, visuals and specifications

Many of the photographs, visuals and videos on this website are illustrations created with digital and AI-assisted tools to present the ESMI boat, its modules and how it can be used. They may show concepts, options or prototypes, and do not necessarily represent a final, approved or delivered boat.

Specifications, layouts, colours, options and performance figures are given for information and may change as the product develops. Only a written offer from ESMI is binding.

The projects and roadmap described in the Portfolio section present ESMI's plans and intended uses; some are planned rather than completed.

Journal, sources and references

The Journal articles share news, field notes and general information about solar-electric boats in the Philippines. Figures and facts from third parties (such as Rappler, MARINA, the Philippine Coast Guard, the Palawan Provincial Tourism Office, the Asian Development Bank and the Philippine Information Agency) are reported as published by those sources at the time of writing, and remain their responsibility.

Information about registration, permits and safety rules is a general overview, not legal advice. Requirements change, so always confirm with MARINA, the Philippine Coast Guard or your local government before operating a boat.
