Solar on the water: how the canopy powers the boat

On an ESMI boat, the roof that shades passengers is also the power plant. Here is how the canopy, the batteries and the electric drive work together to keep energy use as low as possible.

One roof,
two jobs

Marine solar panels on the canopy charge the batteries throughout the day, while the boat is cruising and while it waits at the pier. A battery management system balances every charge and discharge to protect the cells and extend their life, and shore charging tops the batteries up when needed.

Less drag,
less energy

Two slim aluminium hulls slip through the water with less drag than a single wide hull, so the electric drive needs less power to keep the boat moving.

The drive turns stored energy straight into movement, quietly and without exhaust.

. marine solar panels .

Built for
tropical water

Battery energy density, marine solar efficiency and motor efficiency keep improving year after year. We choose marine-grade materials and sealed electrical compartments, and lay out every system so that inspection and battery servicing stay simple for trained local crews.
