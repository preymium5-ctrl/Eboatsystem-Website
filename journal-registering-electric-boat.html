Registering an electric boat in the Philippines

Every boat used commercially in the Philippines needs the right papers. Here is a plain-language overview based on MARINA and Philippine Coast Guard rules. Requirements change, so always confirm with your regional MARINA office before you operate.

The documents

Certificate of Philippine Registry and Certificate of Ownership: required for every domestically owned vessel, regardless of size.

Certificate of Public Convenience (CPC): the franchise MARINA issues for vessels of 20 gross tons or below to operate as a domestic water transport service, which tour operators need for commercial trips.

Boatbuilder registration: manufacturers like ESMI register with MARINA themselves, under Memorandum Circular 2015-09.

Safety
on board

Tourist boats are subject to Philippine Coast Guard safety rules: life vests, passenger limits and regular inspections.

We build compliance into the boat itself, with mounting points for safety equipment, capacity plating and signage, rather than leaving it to the buyer.
