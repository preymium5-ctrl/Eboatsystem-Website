Field notes from Coron, Palawan

Before drawing a single hull, June Delgado and the ESMI team travelled across Coron and the neighbouring island communities of Palawan to meet the people who live from the sea: fishers, boat captains and tour operators.

What we heard

The same concerns came up again and again. Fuel takes a large share of every day's earnings. Engines are hard to repair far from a workshop. And noise and fuel spills affect the reefs and fishing grounds that families depend on, in a region where tourism keeps growing.

Listening
first

Operators did not ask for an unfamiliar boat. They asked for something reliable, simple to run and easy to fix, that their crews could learn quickly.

That became our brief: a boat built from modules, powered by the sun, with parts that can be replaced one by one.

. pilot testing in Coron .

From notebook
to boat

Those conversations shaped every part of the ESMI boat, from the forward-facing seating and simple helm to the training and service we provide at handover. Coron is also where we start: our first pilot deployments with local partners will take place there.
